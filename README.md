## Current Research

### Reliable Multimodal Alzheimer's Prognosis

Developing baseline and landmark survival models that integrate multimodal clinical and biomarker information through a leakage-safe temporal design, with conformal uncertainty calibration, informative-missingness analysis, and external validation for transportability.

### Dynamic Cognitive-State Prediction

Modelling cognitive improvement, persistence, and progression through longitudinal updating and participant-grouped methods, using a locked ADNI-to-OASIS-3 external validation protocol to assess calibration and transportability.

### 3D Alzheimer's MRI Staging

Investigating six diagnostic stages from structural MRI using subject-level splitting, a multi-scale 3D CNN, imbalance-aware learning, robust class-wise evaluation, and model explanations.
